Privacy Notice

Association of Medicine Counter Assistants of Ghana (AMCAG) · Last updated: 22 November 2026

AMCAG respects your privacy and handles your personal data in line with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of Ghana. This notice explains what we collect, why, who can see it and what your rights are.

What we collect

  • Identity and contact details: full name, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender, marital status, residential address and region.
  • Professional details: Pharmacy Council PIN (if you give it), employment status and workplace.
  • Next of kin: name and phone number.
  • Your profile photograph.
  • Membership and payment records: membership number, registration fee and dues status, and payment references. We do not store your mobile money PIN, card details or bank passwords; payments are handled by our payment providers.
  • Your sign-in password, which is handled only by our authentication provider (Google Firebase) in protected form. AMCAG staff cannot see it.

Why we use it

  • To register and verify you as a member and issue your membership ID card.
  • To run membership services, dues, meetings, elections and communication.
  • To keep the association's records and meet legal duties.

Who can see it

  • You can see and update your own record.
  • Authorised AMCAG executives can see member records they need to do their work. Regional executives see only members of their own region.
  • Public verification of your ID card shows only your name, region, membership status and photo.
  • We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the system (for example hosting, authentication, email and payment providers), or where the law requires.

How we protect it

  • Access is limited by role, and sensitive fields are kept in restricted records.
  • Data is stored with Google Firebase over encrypted connections.
  • We keep data only as long as needed for membership and legal purposes.

Your rights

You may ask to see the data we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it where the law allows. You may also withdraw your consent, although we may then be unable to keep your membership active. To make a request, contact us at info@amcag.org. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change.
