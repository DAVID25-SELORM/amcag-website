Safe by design

  • Only verified, approved members can read or write.
  • Your phone number is never shown to other members.
  • There are no private one-to-one messages, so nothing is hidden from moderators.
  • Anyone can report a message. Regional executives and national moderators review reports, and every moderation action is logged.
